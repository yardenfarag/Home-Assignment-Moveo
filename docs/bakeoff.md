## Conclusion (written by hand)
**Winner: `openai/gpt-6.1-terra`** (D110). It passed 39/39 and is about 6× cheaper per answer than Sonnet.

- **How the field was picked.** 9 models were screened with one run each (13 cases). The top 3 plus Sonnet got 2 more runs
  each. The 5 models screened out (the rows with 13 case-runs) either broke a "never wrong" promise (a wrong number, top
  hub or premise verdict) or were too slow (p95 over 30 s). DeepSeek, MiniMax and Mistral fall in the first group, both
  Geminis in the second.
- **The judge question.** Luna (OpenAI) grades the main table. On the same answers it failed Anthropic models 17 times and
  OpenAI models 0 times. The independent Gemini judge failed Anthropic models 3 times. So Luna is harsher on Anthropic's
  longer, more hedged answers. **The winner doesn't change:** with Gemini's grades, Terra (39), Sol (38) and Sonnet (37)
  tie, and Terra is the cheapest. Going forward the judge is Gemini, since the judge must come from a different provider
  than the answering model (D111, proposed).
- **Judges that failed validation.** Qwen and DeepSeek flipped their 1–5 scale ("pass, the assistant correctly…",
  faithful 1/5). The report now flags such judges as unreliable automatically (D109).
- **Caveats.** 3 runs per finalist is still a small sample. The prompt was developed on Sonnet and never tuned for Terra
  (same prompt for all, B5). Terra is the slowest finalist on average (12.8 s), though p95 20.9 s is inside the limit.
  Confirmation run on the final setup: 13/13, $0.147 for the whole set ([evals.md](evals.md)).
